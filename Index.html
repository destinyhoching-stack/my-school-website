What is Keystone

When we say the Book of Mormon is the keystone of our religion, we mean it is one of the most important foundations of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.


Joseph Smith taught that the Book of Mormon is the keystone of our religion in three important ways:


1. The keystone of our testimony of Jesus Christ. The Book of Mormon testifies that Jesus Christ is our Savior and Redeemer. It teaches us about His love, His Atonement, and His plan for us.


2. The keystone of our doctrine. The Book of Mormon teaches the fullness of the gospel of Jesus Christ and helps us understand Heavenly Father's plan for His children.


3. The keystone of our testimony of the Restoration. If the Book of Mormon is true, then Joseph Smith was a prophet of God, and the Church was restored through him. This helps us know that God restored His gospel and priesthood authority to the earth.


Think of it like an arch: A keystone is the central stone that holds an arch together. Without it, the arch would not stand the way it was designed to. In the same way, the Book of Mormon is central to our faith because it helps support our testimony of Jesus Christ and the Restoration of His Church.
